Assignment 3

A Shared Amenity Requires Shared Responsibility

Introduction to this artifact

Assignment focus: A community-focused argument about the loss of the shared laundry-room amenity at Magnolia.

Why I selected it: This essay demonstrates that literacy skills transfer beyond literary analysis. It uses a clear position, concrete examples, cause-and-effect reasoning, and an audience-aware tone to explain why shared spaces require responsibility and respect.

What I learned: I practiced organizing a real-world concern into a persuasive argument and explaining how individual actions can affect an entire community.

Complete assignment

The laundry room at Magnolia was provided as a convenient amenity for residents, allowing everyone to wash and dry their clothes without having to pay extra. Unfortunately, because of the inconsiderate behavior and lack of cleanliness displayed by certain residents, this privilege has been taken away. What was supposed to be a helpful service for the entire community has turned into an unpleasant environment due to people failing to respect the shared space. It is disappointing that something beneficial to everyone had to be taken away because some residents could not follow basic rules or show common courtesy.

One of the biggest problems with the laundry room has been the lack of consideration shown by residents. Washing machines and dryers are shared by everyone, meaning people should be respectful of others who also need to use them. Instead, some residents leave their clothes sitting in machines for hours, preventing others from doing their laundry. Others fail to remove their belongings in a timely manner or leave machines occupied when they are no longer using them. This behavior shows a lack of respect for other people’s time and demonstrates an attitude that their own convenience matters more than everyone else’s.

Even worse is the lack of cleanliness. A shared laundry room should be kept sanitary and presentable, but some residents treat it as if nobody else has to use it. Leaving behind lint, detergent spills, trash, and other messes creates an unpleasant environment for everyone. It is not someone else’s responsibility to clean up after adults who are perfectly capable of cleaning up after themselves. Basic hygiene and common courtesy should not be difficult expectations to meet, especially when using a facility that is provided for the entire community.

The consequences of these actions affect every resident, including those who have consistently treated the laundry room with respect. Losing free laundry means residents now have to deal with an additional expense and inconvenience because other people could not be responsible. It is unfair to punish an entire community for the actions of individuals who refuse to take care of something they all benefit from. Unfortunately, when people repeatedly abuse a privilege, everyone eventually has to deal with the consequences.

At the end of the day, living in a community requires cooperation, responsibility, and respect. Nobody should have to clean up after someone else or wait unnecessarily because another resident cannot be considerate. The laundry room was a free amenity intended to make life easier for everyone at Magnolia, but the actions of careless and inconsiderate residents have ruined that opportunity. Residents need to understand that shared spaces are not personal spaces and that having access to something for free does not mean they can treat it however they want. Hopefully, this situation serves as a reminder that cleanliness, respect, and basic consideration are necessary when living around other people. A little responsibility from everyone could have prevented this entire situation.
